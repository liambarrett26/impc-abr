# Gene Set Enrichment Analysis: Results Summary

## Overview

We tested whether genes identified as auditory phenodeviants by our Bayesian mixture model (133 genes, 139 alleles with BF >= 3) are enriched for other phenotypic categories across the IMPC dataset. The analysis uses Fisher's exact tests with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction, applied at every level of the Mammalian Phenotype (MP) ontology hierarchy.

**Foreground**: 133 unique genes with Bayes Factor >= 3 (substantial evidence of hearing loss)
**Background**: 6,549 unique genes analysed in the Bayesian pipeline (complete ABR data, met inclusion criteria)
**Phenotype data source**: IMPC genotype-phenotype assertions (67,350 significant calls across all phenotyping procedures, Data Release 22.1)
**MP ontology**: 14,695 terms, 19,487 parent-child relationships (OBO release 2025-03-19)

### Two-pass analysis design

The analysis is structured in two passes to allow both comparability with prior literature and methodologically rigorous assessment of circularity:

**Pass 1 — Unfiltered enrichment (Stages 2 and 3)**: Enrichment is computed using all IMPC genotype-phenotype assertions, replicating the approach used by Vicencio-Jimenez et al. (2026). Enriched terms are labelled by a two-pass classifier (keyword/ontology + procedure-based) as acoustic-dependent, vestibular, or independent — but no assertions are removed. This allows direct comparison with published OR values.

**Pass 2 — Assertion-level filtered enrichment (Stage 3b)**: Individual gene-to-term assertions generated by acoustic-dependent procedures are removed at source before gene-to-MP mappings are rebuilt and enrichment is re-run from scratch. Terms that are partially acoustic-dependent retain their non-acoustic evidence, while terms that are entirely acoustic-derived naturally drop to zero foreground genes and disappear. The change between Pass 1 and Pass 2 quantifies the circularity in each enrichment result.

### Comparison with Vicencio-Jimenez et al. (2026)

Vicencio-Jimenez report for their 331 IMPC-annotated hearing loss genes (Data Release 23.0):

- Behavioral: OR=3.13 — we find OR=4.64
- Cognitive: OR=1.98 — we find abnormal cognition OR=2.25 at the sub-branch level
- Emotional: OR=2.11 — we find abnormal emotion/affect OR=2.27 at the sub-branch level

Our analysis additionally identifies skeleton (OR=2.83), reproductive (OR=2.00), homeostasis/metabolism (OR=1.57), and immune (OR=1.60) enrichments not reported by Vicencio-Jimenez.

## Stage 3b: Assertion-Level Acoustic Filtering

Rather than classifying whole MP terms and applying an arbitrary threshold, we filter at the individual assertion level:

- **Fully acoustic procedures** (ACS, ABR, ESLIM_011): all 2,774 assertions removed
- **Mixed procedures** (CSD/SHIRPA): 253 assertions with acoustic-specific MP terms (pinna reflex, startle) removed; 3,728 non-acoustic assertions (gait, coat morphology, posture) retained
- **All other procedures**: all 60,595 assertions retained
- **Total removed**: 3,027 of 67,350 assertions (4.5%)
- **Remaining**: 64,323 assertions

Gene-to-MP mappings are rebuilt from scratch on the filtered data and enrichment re-run.

### Top-level: full vs assertion-filtered comparison

| MP Category | Full OR | Full a | Filtered OR | Filtered a | Full sig? | Filtered sig? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nervous system | **4.55** | **42** | **1.02** | **7** | YES | **no** |
| behavior/neurological | 4.64 | 95 | 2.49 | 72 | YES | YES |
| hearing/vestibular/ear | 123.29 | 106 | 25.40 | 7 | YES | YES |
| skeleton | 2.83 | 48 | 2.83 | 48 | YES | YES |
| reproductive system | 2.00 | 33 | 2.00 | 33 | YES | YES |
| homeostasis/metabolism | 1.57 | 69 | 1.57 | 69 | YES | YES |
| immune system | 1.60 | 44 | 1.60 | 44 | YES | YES |

The nervous system enrichment collapses entirely (OR 4.55 → 1.02, a drops from 42 to 7). The behavioral enrichment is partially reduced (OR 4.64 → 2.49, a drops from 95 to 72 as 23 genes lose their acoustic-derived behavioral assertions) but remains significant. Skeleton, reproductive, metabolic, and immune enrichments are completely unaffected — none of their evidence comes from acoustic procedures.

### Filtered hierarchical enrichment

143 significant terms survive assertion-level filtering (down from 157). The 14 terms that drop out are exactly the acoustic-dependent terms identified by the classifier: all ABR/hearing terms, startle/PPI terms, pinna reflex, and their procedure-driven parent terms (synaptic transmission, nervous system physiology, abnormal reflex).

Key terms that survive with reduced gene counts:

| Term | Full a | Filtered a | Filtered OR | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| abnormal motor capabilities/coordination/movement | 87 | 58 | 2.41 | Open field, grip strength, SHIRPA (non-acoustic items) |
| abnormal behavior | 95 | 72 | 2.49 | Open field, light-dark, fear conditioning |
| abnormal involuntary movement | 70 | — | dropped | Was 90% acoustic |
| abnormal reflex | 69 | — | dropped | Was 96% acoustic |

All skeleton, metabolic, immune, and reproductive terms retain identical gene counts — they had zero acoustic contributions.

## Stage 4: Centre-Matched Sensitivity Analysis

Centre-matched results (only counting MP assertions from the same centre as the gene's ABR data) are consistent with the unmatched analysis:

| MP Category | Unmatched OR | Centre-matched OR | Centre-matched sig? |
|---|---|---|---|
| hearing/vestibular/ear | 123.29 | 123.94 | YES |
| behavior/neurological | 4.64 | 4.57 | YES |
| nervous system | 4.55 | 4.50 | YES |
| skeleton | 2.83 | 2.71 | YES |
| reproductive system | 2.00 | 2.01 | YES |
| homeostasis/metabolism | 1.57 | 1.62 | YES |
| immune system | 1.60 | 1.64 | YES |
| adipose tissue | 1.88 (NS) | 1.92 | YES (p_adj=0.043) |

Centre matching slightly strengthens some effects and brings adipose tissue to significance. The enrichment patterns are not driven by cross-centre confounding. The high match rate (47,669 matched vs 1,767 unmatched assertions, 96.4%) reflects the IMPC's standardised pipeline where most phenotyping occurs at the same centre.
